# Top 10 Tax Deductions for Finger Lakes Landlords in 2026

As a landlord in the beautiful Finger Lakes region, you work hard to maintain your properties and provide great homes for your tenants. The good news? Many of your regular expenses are tax-deductible.

Here are the top 10 deductions you should be tracking this year:

1. **Mortgage Interest** – Deduct interest paid on loans for your rental properties.
2. **Property Taxes** – Fully deductible for rental real estate.
3. **Insurance Premiums** – Homeowners, liability, and flood insurance all qualify.
4. **Repairs & Maintenance** – Painting, plumbing, roofing, landscaping, snow removal.
5. **Utilities** – If you pay water, heat, electric, or trash for tenants.
6. **Depreciation** – Spread the cost of your property over 27.5 years (huge saver!).
7. **Mileage & Travel** – Trips to properties, supply runs, or landlord meetings.
8. **Professional Fees** – Bookkeeping (like Finger Lakes Ledgers!), legal, property management.
9. **Advertising** – Listing fees, signs, online ads to find tenants.
10. **Home Office** – Portion of your home used exclusively for managing rentals.

Proper tracking of these expenses can save thousands at tax time.
